# First 10 DineroMundo users

## Initial audience

Start narrowly with Spanish-speaking freelancers and small service businesses in Mexico, Colombia and Peru: cleaners, repair professionals, contractors, consultants and small agencies. Expand only after their terminology and workflows are understood.

## Free outreach plan

1. Invite 3 people from the founder's existing professional or personal network.
2. Make 3 helpful, non-promotional posts in relevant Facebook or local-business communities after checking each community's rules.
3. Send 10 individualized LinkedIn messages to known or clearly relevant small-business owners; no scraping or automation.
4. Share one practical DineroMundo guide with its relevant free tool, then mention the business workspace as an optional next step.
5. Ask each active tester for one referral only after they have successfully created a customer and document.

Never mass-message, scrape contact lists or automate WhatsApp/community outreach.

## First-session walkthrough

Homepage → create account → confirm email → onboarding → customer → quote → invoice → income/expense → dashboard → PDF → backup.

## Manual feedback

Ask after the first completed workflow:

- Was signup easy?
- Which feature did you use?
- What was confusing?
- Would you use DineroMundo weekly?
- What is missing from your normal workflow?
- Would you pay USD $9 per month?
- What specific result would make it worth paying for?

Track responses in a private, minimal spreadsheet without passwords, financial records or unnecessary personal data.

## Metrics available without third-party tracking

- Auth account count and monthly active users.
- Businesses with recent record activity.
- Aggregate counts of customers, quotes, invoices, incomes and expenses.
- Return activity inferred from record timestamps or Auth activity.
- Aggregate automation/recurrence adoption and plan-limit errors.

Use aggregate operational queries only. Do not inspect customer content for marketing and do not add third-party trackers without approval.

## Payment gate

Keep payments off. Reconsider a payment-provider phase only after several real users return weekly and at least 3–5 explicitly say they would pay for the current product or a clearly defined improvement.
